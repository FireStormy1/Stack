# STACK — Rules

## 1. Objective

Build the tallest tower possible by accurately stacking each moving block on top of the previous block.

The better your timing, the wider and more stable your tower remains.

Your goal is simple:

> **Stack higher. Score higher. Beat your best.**

---

## 2. How to Play

A block continuously moves from side to side above the tower.

### Mobile

**Tap anywhere on the game screen** to drop the block.

### Desktop

Press:

**SPACE**

to drop the block.

Try to align the moving block with the block below it.

---

## 3. Stacking

When the block lands:

* The overlapping portion stays.
* The non-overlapping portion falls away.
* The remaining portion becomes the next platform.
* The tower grows by one level.

The more accurately you place the block, the larger your next platform remains.

---

## 4. Missing a Block

If the moving block does not overlap the tower at all, the run ends.

A small mistake does not immediately end the game.

You only lose the part that does not overlap.

---

## 5. Perfect

A **Perfect** happens when the moving block is placed extremely accurately on the previous block.

Perfect placements:

* Continue your Perfect streak
* Trigger special feedback
* Build your streak bonus
* Produce evolving streak audio

Try to keep your streak going for as long as possible.

---

## 6. Perfect Streak

Every consecutive Perfect placement increases your streak.

Examples:

```text id="f3nqiw"
PERFECT ×1
PERFECT ×2
PERFECT ×3
...
PERFECT ×10
...
PERFECT ×50
...
PERFECT ×100
```

There is no maximum streak.

If you make a normal placement, your Perfect streak resets.

---

## 7. Streak Bonus

Every 10 consecutive Perfect placements increases your streak bonus by **+5**.

```text id="qf52ef"
10 Perfects → +5
20 Perfects → +10
30 Perfects → +15
40 Perfects → +20
50 Perfects → +25
```

If your streak breaks, the bonus resets.

Your next 10 Perfect placements will start again at **+5**.

---

## 8. Score

Every successful stack gives you points.

Your base score increases as you reach major milestones.

Initial progression:

```text id="v0mx9w"
0–99       → +1
100–249    → +2
250–499    → +3
500–749    → +4
750–999    → +5
1000+      → +6
```

Your Perfect streak bonus is added separately.

---

## 9. Milestones

Major milestones provide additional progression.

Initial milestones:

**100 · 250 · 500 · 750 · 1000**

More milestones can continue beyond 1000.

Reaching a milestone does **not** reset your Perfect streak.

Your streak continues normally.

---

## 10. Relaxed Mode

Relaxed Mode is designed for chilling out.

Enjoy:

* Slower movement
* Calm progression
* Relaxing music
* Atmospheric backgrounds
* Minimal visuals

The speed gradually increases as your score grows, but the increases remain small.

Relaxed Mode is about enjoying the experience rather than feeling pressured.

---

## 11. Competitive Mode

Competitive Mode is designed for players who want to push their score.

The block starts moving slowly but becomes progressively faster.

Initial speed increases begin around:

**40 → 80 → 120 → ...**

The speed continues increasing gradually.

Even at very high scores, the game should remain readable and controllable.

---

## 12. Backgrounds

STACK contains multiple atmospheric environments.

Each run can randomly select a background.

The environments follow the game's:

**Sunset / Terracotta**

visual identity.

Backgrounds may include:

* Sunset skies
* Mountains
* Hills
* Desert
* Ocean
* Clouds
* Twilight
* Night

The background should never interfere with gameplay visibility.

---

## 13. Music

STACK contains multiple relaxing music tracks.

Music can change between runs.

The music is designed to complement the selected environment and create a calm atmosphere.

You can mute the game at any time.

---

## 14. Controls

### Mobile

**Tap** → Drop block

### Desktop

**SPACE** → Drop block

**ESC** → Pause / Menu

**M** → Mute / Unmute

---

## 15. Pause

Press the Pause button to temporarily stop the game.

You can then:

* Resume
* Restart
* Return to the appropriate menu

The tower and game state remain unchanged while paused.

---

## 16. Game Over

The game ends when a block completely misses the tower.

After Game Over:

* Your final tower is revealed.
* The camera zooms out.
* Your score is displayed.
* Your best score is displayed.
* Milestone information may be shown.
* You can capture/share your result.
* You can try again.

---

## 17. Best Score

Your highest score is saved locally.

If you beat your previous record, STACK will show a subtle **New Best** indication.

Keep stacking to beat your personal record.

---

## 18. Share Your Tower

After Game Over, you can capture your completed tower and share your result.

The result should preserve:

* Your tower
* Your score
* Your best score
* The current environment
* STACK's visual identity

---

## 19. The Golden Rule

There is only one real rule:

> **Don't stop stacking.**
